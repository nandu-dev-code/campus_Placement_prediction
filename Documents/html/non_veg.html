NON-VEG

Non-vegetarian food, often referred to as non-veg, includes meat, poultry, fish, and other animal products. It is a significant source of protein and essential nutrients for many people around the world. Non-veg dishes can be prepared in various ways, such as grilling, frying, baking, or stewing.
